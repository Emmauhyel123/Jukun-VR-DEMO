The VR Experience

Inside the Virtual Reality

Put on your headset and be transported to the court of the Aku Uka. Participate, observe, and learn the history behind every movement.

360° Environment

Meticulously recreated environments of Wukari, including the festival grounds and traditional architecture.

Interactive Dancing

Follow the rhythm. Use your VR controllers to match the gestures of the Keku dancers and learn the steps.

Historical Context

Interactive nodes provide spoken history about the significance of the music, the attire, and the Kwararafa lineage.

A Single Session

How a Visit Unfolds

  1. 1

    Arrive at the festival grounds

    You step into the 360° recreation of Wukari, with the Aku Uka's court set around you.

  2. 2

    Watch the Keku dancers

    Motion-captured performers move through the ceremony at full scale, with spatial audio recorded live.

  3. 3

    Match the gestures yourself

    Pick up the rhythm with your controllers and follow along with the steps in real time.

  4. 4

    Learn the history behind it

    Tap interactive nodes for spoken history on the attire, the music, and the Kwararafa lineage.
