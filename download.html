Download the App

Get the executable for a compatible PC, or transfer it to your standalone VR headset (Meta Quest 2/3/Pro or Pico 4).

Requires a VR-ready PC or SideQuest for installation on standalone headsets.

System Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10/11
  • GPU: GTX 1060 or better
  • RAM: 8GB minimum
  • Storage: 2GB available
  • Standalone: Quest 2/3/Pro
Installation Guide

From Download to Headset

  1. 1

    Download the .zip

    Get the 1.2GB package above and extract it to a folder on your PC.

  2. 2

    Choose your setup

    Run it directly on a VR-ready PC, or sideload it onto a standalone headset with SideQuest.

  3. 3

    Put on your headset

    Launch JukunVR from your library and you'll arrive at the festival grounds.

  4. 4

    Need a hand?

    Reach the team on the contact page for setup help.
